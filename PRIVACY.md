# Privacy Policy: Annoying Client

- **No tracking.** Annoying Client does not collect analytics, usage statistics or personal data.
- **Sign-in tokens.** If you sign in for Direct launch, your Microsoft and Minecraft tokens are stored only on your computer, encrypted with Windows DPAPI. They are sent only to Microsoft, Xbox Live and Minecraft services to sign you in and start the game. Sign out in the launcher, or delete `%APPDATA%\.annoyingclient\account.dat`, to remove them.
- **Network use.** The launcher connects to Mojang (game files and Java), Fabric (the mod loader) and Modrinth (mods you choose to install), and GitHub to check for launcher updates.
- **Cosmetics.** In game, the client signs in to the Annoying Client cosmetics server (`annoying-client.onrender.com`) the same way you join a Minecraft server, so it can show capes and trails. The server stores your Minecraft username and UUID, your coins and your cosmetics. Your Microsoft password and tokens are never sent to it. Set the Cosmetics module's Server URL to `off` to turn this off.
